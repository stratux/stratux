The GNSS page provides detailed status of the GPS/GNSS receiver and satellite constellation.

GNSS Status shows a map of the current position alongside grouped information panels: position (latitude, longitude, altitude), fix quality and satellite counts, accuracy metrics, and motion and time data.

Accuracy metrics include:

  • H/V Accuracy — estimated horizontal and vertical position error.
  • HDOP / PDOP — horizontal and position dilution of precision. Lower values indicate a better satellite geometry.
  • NACp — Navigation Accuracy Category for position, per AC 20-165A. Values range from 0 (EPU ≥ 18.52 km) to 11 (EPU < 3 m).

Constellation Plot shows satellite positions in the sky as a polar view. Elevation rings are drawn at 0°, 30°, and 60°. Filled dots indicate satellites currently used in the position solution; hollow dots are tracked but not in solution.

Signal Strength shows a bar chart of signal levels (dB-Hz) per satellite. The Y-axis scales automatically to the strongest signal received. Dimmed bars are satellites not in the current solution.

Satellites are color-coded by constellation: GPS (blue), GLONASS (red), Galileo (green), BeiDou (orange), SBAS (purple), QZSS (cyan).

Use the Show satellites not in solution checkbox to include tracked and seen satellites in all views.

The map supports offline use when local map tiles (MBTiles) are installed on the Stratux device. Use the layer switcher icon on the map to toggle between available tile sources.
