The AHRS page shows the current attitude and acceleration data from the onboard inertial sensor.

Attitude Indicator displays an artificial horizon with pitch and roll derived from the AHRS sensor. Heading is shown at the bottom and is derived from GPS track in degrees true. Press the AHRS title to toggle fullscreen mode.

G-Meter shows current, minimum, and maximum G-load recorded during the session. Press Reset to clear the min/max values.

The Cage AHRS button levels the attitude indication when the aircraft is in straight-and-level flight. The AHRS sensor orientation must be configured once via the Settings page before first use — press Calibrate AHRS Sensors and follow the on-screen instructions. Re-calibration is only needed if the physical orientation of the sensor in the aircraft changes.
